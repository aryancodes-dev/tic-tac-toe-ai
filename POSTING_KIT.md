## Full description (README / portfolio / LinkedIn post)
Tic-Tac-Toe v5 is Project #02 of my 100+ AI Projects series, upgraded — nothing from before was removed.

You still play against a Minimax AI that never loses on Impossible mode (best you can do is draw), plus Easy and Medium, and a Vs Friend mode. v5 adds: Hard difficulty, play as X or O, choose who moves first, a Hint button that shows your best square, Undo, streaks and win rate saved on your phone, a move log and match history, sound on/off, light/dark theme, keyboard play (1-9), and an end-game banner that explains the result.

The web version installs as an app on your phone, and the Python version has the exact same v5 Minimax brain, difficulties and undo.

## Tech stack

## Features
1. Unbeatable Minimax AI on Impossible, plus Hard (85% best), Medium (50% best), Easy (random)
2. Play as X or O, AI first or you first, Vs Friend mode
3. Hint, Undo, streaks, win rate, move log, match history, sound, light/dark theme
4. Website + installable phone app + Python version
