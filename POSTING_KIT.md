## Full description (README / portfolio / LinkedIn post)
Tic-Tac-Toe v6 is Project #02 of my 100+ AI Projects series, upgraded — nothing from v5 was removed.

You still play against a Minimax AI that never loses on Impossible mode on 3×3 (best you can do is draw), plus Easy, Medium and Hard, and a Vs Friend mode, with Hint, Undo, streaks, history, sound and theme. v6 adds board sizes: 3×3, 4×4, 5×5, 6×6, 9×9, 12×12 and 15×15. Win with 3 in a row on 3×3, 4 on 4×4, and 5 on 5×5 and bigger — 15×15 is Gomoku-style. On big boards the AI uses a strong heuristic instead of perfect Minimax, because perfect Minimax on 15×15 would hang a phone.

The web version installs as an app on your phone, and the Python version has the exact same v5 Minimax brain, difficulties and undo.

## Tech stack
HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Minimax AI, PWA, localStorage, Python

## Features
1. Board sizes: 3×3, 4×4, 5×5, 6×6, 9×9, 12×12, 15×15 — 3/4/5 in a row to win
2. Unbeatable Minimax AI on 3×3 Impossible, plus Hard (85% best), Medium (50% best), Easy (random); strong heuristic AI on big boards
3. Play as X or O, AI first or you first, Vs Friend mode
4. Hint, Undo, streaks, win rate, move log, match history, sound, light/dark theme
5. Website + installable phone app + Python version
